## 1. Triceps Pressdown

**Përgatitja** (ace_pushdown, stoppani_rope)
- ACE, Triceps Pushdowns, "Starting position" [D]: "attachment above head height ... full grip"; "feet hip-width apart or staggered"; "brace your abs ... natural arch in your low back ... torso vertical"; "pull your shoulders back and down"; "Lower the handle or rope until your elbows are at mid-torso, upper arms firmly at your sides, wrists neutral." https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/185/triceps-pushdowns/
- Stoppani, Rope Triceps Pressdown (HK), "Setup" [D]: knees slightly bent, feet shoulder-width, overhand grip near the stoppers, elbows tucked, "pull your shoulder blades back and down, and lift your chest." https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/rope-triceps-pressdown-exercise

**Lëvizja** (ace_pushdown, stoppani_rope)
- ACE "Movement" [D]: "Exhale and slowly extend your elbows, pressing the handle or rope down toward the floor"; "extend fully without locking out. Pause briefly"; "until your elbows begin drifting forward of your mid-line (around upper chest level)". [V] për fjalinë "mid-line (around upper chest level)".
- Stoppani "Movement" [D]: "spread the rope ends apart for a stronger contraction"; "Pause at full extension and squeeze your triceps."

**Tempo dhe fryma** (wilk, korakakis, ace_pushdown): tempo si më sipër; frymë: ACE "Exhale and slowly extend" [D]. "Merre ndërsa e kthen lart" është e anasjella e saj [S].

**Sinjalet** (ace_pushdown, stoppani_rope): nga përgatitja dhe lëvizja më sipër [D]/[S] (shkurtime).

**Gabimet**
- Bërrylat përpara: ACE "Cautions and tips" [D]: "Watch for forward movement of your elbows" (faqja thotë se ndërhyn shpina e gjerë dhe tricepsi punon më pak).
- Vrulli: ACE, Best Triceps Exercises [D]: autorët e lidhën aktivitetin më të ulët të pushdown-eve pjesërisht me lehtësinë e përdorimit të vrullit. https://www.acefitness.org/certifiednewsarticle/3008/ace-study-identifies-best-triceps-exercises/
- Shpatullat/krahët lëvizin: ACE "keeping your torso, shoulders, arms, and wrists in position" [D].
- Kyçet: ACE "wrists neutral (in line with your forearms)" [D].
- Shtrirja: ACE "extend fully without locking out" [D].

**Këshillat**
- Stoppani (HK, "Tips") [D]: "At the bottom of the rep, rotate your forearms so your palms face back, and press down with your thumbs. This shifts emphasis toward the lateral head." Hapur te faqja e librit, jo video.
- ACE studimi [D]: 15 gra; rope pushdown 74, bar pushdown 67, overhead extension 76, lying barbell 62 (% e triangle push-up-it). Burimi: https://www.acefitness.org/certifiednewsarticle/3008/ace-study-identifies-best-triceps-exercises/ Kujdes: studim i vetëm, kampion i vogël (n = 15); u shkrua si fakt i studimit, jo si rekomandim.
- Kholinne 2018 [V] abstrakti: "At 0° shoulder elevation, the long head of the triceps brachii generates a significantly higher muscle force and muscle activation than the lateral and medial heads (p < 0.05). While at 90°, 135° and 180° shoulder elevation, the medial head ... showed a significantly higher muscle force." https://europepmc.org/article/MED/29503079 (10 të rritur).

**Siguria**
- NASM, Corrective Exercise Red Flags [D]: "burning during high-effort sets is a common training response"; "Sharp or sudden pain is a warning sign ... stop the exercise"; "Tingling, numbness, or radiating sensations ... stop and recommend medical evaluation." https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/corrective-exercise-red-flags
- ACSM, A Road Map to Effective Muscle Recovery [D]: "You should stop participation in any exercise activity that causes pain." https://www.acsm.org/docs/default-source/files-for-resource-library/a-road-map-to-effective-muscle-recovery.pdf
- "Ule peshën kur bërrylat nuk qëndrojnë" [S] mbi ACE "Proper technique is important to emphasize the triceps."

**Pak histori:** null (asnjë burim historik i hapur).

## 2. Lying Triceps Extension (shufër EZ)

**Përgatitja** (ace_lying, stoppani_lying)
- ACE, Lying Barbell Triceps Extensions, "Starting position" [D]: "Lie on your back on a flat bench with your feet firmly on the floor"; "Press the bar off your chest until your elbows are fully extended and the bar sits directly above your face"; "neutral wrist position"; Tips: "Keep your upper arms vertical to the floor", "Keep your elbows about shoulder-width apart". https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/36/lying-barbell-triceps-extensions/
- Stoppani, "How To Do The Lying Triceps Extension Exercise", përshkrimi i videos [D] (transkripti nuk është te faqja): EZ-curl bar, duart pak të pronuara, lehtëson kyçet dhe e zhvendos theksin te koka anësore; "lay back on the bench with your arms perpendicular to your chest." https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_b0IsrjMUDQ

**Lëvizja** (ace_lying) [D]: "Inhale and slowly bend your elbows, lowering the bar under control toward your forehead or slightly behind your head"; "Stop when the bar is just above your forehead, or when your upper arms begin to drift backward"; "Exhale and slowly return to the starting position."

**Tempo dhe fryma** (wilk, korakakis, ace_lying): inhale poshtë, exhale lart nga ACE [D].

**Gabimet**: krahët mbrapa (ACE "Do not let the lowering continue once your upper arms start drifting backward" [D]), kyçet (ACE "no bending at the wrist" [D]; Stoppani: EZ "eases wrist stress" [D]), bërrylat (ACE tip), vrulli ("Move slowly and with control in both directions" [D]).

**Këshillat**
- Stoppani (përshkrimi i videos) [D]: "To emphasize the long head, extend the arms farther back so the bar travels above the head. This keeps tension consistent ... and increases the stretch on the long head."
- ACE studimi [D]: lying barbell 62, overhead extension 76, rope pushdown 74 (% e triangle push-up-it, n = 15).
- Korakakis et al. [V]: ROM që "emphasizes training at long muscle lengths", tempo 2–8 s. Shprehja "brenda kufirit që e mban formën të pastër" është e imja [S].

**Siguria**
- NSCA 2017, Standard 3.2 [V]: "attentive spotting must be provided for participants performing activities in which free weights are supported on the trunk" or "moved over the head/face." https://dxpprod.nsca.com/globalassets/education/nsca_strength_and_conditioning_professional_standards_and_guidelines.pdf
- ACE: "Do not let the lowering continue once your upper arms start drifting backward" [D].
- NASM dhe ACSM si te pika 1.

## 4. Cable Overhead Triceps Extension (litar)

**Përgatitja dhe lëvizja** (ace_kovar, maeo)
- ACE (Kovar), "Cable Rope Extension" [D]: "Use a split-foot stance and hinge slightly at the hips to maintain a neutral spine. 'Bring the rope overhead and keep your elbows bent.' Extend the elbows, splitting the rope at full extension, then return overhead while keeping tension on the cable."
- Maeo 2023 [V] abstrakti: "Using a cable machine, 21 adults conducted elbow extensions (90-0°) with one arm in the overhead (Overhead-Arm) and the other arm in the neutral (Neutral-Arm) position." https://europepmc.org/article/MED/35819335

**Gabimet**: shpina (ACE: "neutral spine" [D]); krahët e sipërm lëvizin ("elbow extensions (90-0°) ... overhead position" [V], [S]); tensioni (ACE "keeping tension on the cable" [D]); "lëvizje e shkurtuar" mbështetet te Korakakis [V] ("emphasizes training at long muscle lengths") dhe Maeo.

**Këshillat**
- Maeo 2023 [V]: "Changes in muscle volume in Overhead-Arm compared to Neutral-Arm were 1.5-fold greater for the TB Long (+28.5% vs. +19.6%) ... 1.4-fold greater for the Whole-TB (+19.9% vs. +13.9%)"; "their absolute values/weights were always lower in Overhead-Arm (-34-39%)". Studimi është me 21 të rritur dhe 12 javë; teksti i app-it e thotë këtë. Burimi i hapur: europepmc.org/article/MED/35819335. Wiley (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1080/17461391.2022.2100279) nuk u hap (client_error).
- Kholinne [V] si te pika 1.
- ACE studimi [D] si te pika 1.

**Siguria**: ACE (Kovar) "neutral spine" [D]; "nëse harkohet, ule peshën" [S]; NASM dhe ACSM si më sipër.

## 5. Dumbbell Overhead Triceps Extension (një dumbbell, dy duar)

- ACE, Triceps Extension (dumbbell, ID 74) [D]: "Stand in a split stance holding one dumbbell with both hands"; "Brace your core and depress and retract your shoulder blades"; "extending your elbows until your upper arms are vertical to the floor. Elbows point forward and stay not completely locked"; "Stop when your elbows reach about a 90-degree bend or when your upper arms start moving backward"; cautions: "Don't let the dumbbell contact the back of your head", "Keep your head, torso, upper arms, wrists, and feet still"; tips: "elbows about shoulder-width apart"; frymë: "Exhale and slowly press ... Inhale and let the weight slowly bend your elbows." https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/74/triceps-extension/
- Maeo, Korakakis, Kholinne si te pika 4. NSCA Standard 3.2 [V] për pesha mbi kokë ose fytyrë. Dumbbell-i lëviz pas kokës, jo mbi fytyrë; zbatimi i standardit është gjykimi im [S] (shih pyetjet).
