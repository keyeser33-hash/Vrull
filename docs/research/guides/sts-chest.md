Të gjitha burimet u hapën më 15.11.2026 (WebFetch, curl ose Europe PMC). Citatet janë në gjuhën e burimit, deri në 25 fjalë. ## 1. Bench Press (teksti i bench-press.md, i pandryshuar)
- **NASM, Barbell Bench Press** https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/barbell-bench-press. Mbështet setup 1–3, hapat, gabimet 1–3 dhe 5.
  > "Lie on a flat bench with your back firmly against the backrest and your feet flat on the floor."
  > "Grip the bar with hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart."
  > "Stop just above your chest to maintain continuous muscular tension throughout the set."
  > "Lowering the bar too quickly, which reduces time under tension and increases injury risk"
- **ACE, Chest Press** https://acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/5/chest-press. Mbështet setup 5, hapat.
  > "Press the feet into the ground and the hips into the bench while lifting the bar off the rack."
- **Stoppani, Bench Press Master Class** https://jimstoppani.com/training/bench-press-master-class
  > "maintaining five points of contact: feet, glutes, upper back, head, and hands."
  > "Your elbows should be pointed forward with your upper arms at around a 45-degree angle to your torso."
  > "Touch your chest lightly with the bar, then press it back up in a slight backward arcing motion"
- **Faigenbaum, Westcott** https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/a-step-by-step-guide-for-a-proper-bench-press
  > "A spotter should be behind the lifter's head."
  > "The hips should remain on the bench"

Shënim për Bench Press (seksioni "Pak histori"): burimi thotë "Among earlier photos of benches with uprights", prandaj teksti thotë "një nga fotot e hershme".

## 2. Incline Bench Press
- **NASM, Incline Barbell Bench Press** https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/incline-barbell-bench-press (setup, hapat, gabimet).
  > "Set a bench to a 45-degree incline angle."
  > "Press the bar upward and slightly forward in a controlled manner"
  > "Bench angle too steep (above 45 degrees), which shifts emphasis to anterior deltoid"
  > "Lower the bar with control back toward your upper chest, maintaining tension in the chest muscles."
- Setup jep vetëm atë që thotë NASM (45°). Mospërputhja e këndit është te një tip me emrat: NASM 45° (mbi 45° gabim), ACE (presa me shtanga) 45–60°, Stoppani (kapje e kundërt) 30–45°, Rodríguez-Ridao 30°. Të tre ushtrimet e pjerrëta (Incline Bench Press, Incline Dumbbell Press, Reverse-Grip) kanë këtë tip.
- Lauver: "26–50% e fazës koncentrike" (abstrakti thotë "26-50% contraction duration" brenda fazës koncentrike).
- **Stoppani, Master Chest Workouts** https://www.jimstoppani.com/training/chest-workouts
  > "Chances are, you're already doing incline presses to focus on the upper pecs."
  > "great exercise for building overall mass and thickness up top"

## 3. Incline Dumbbell Press
- **NASM, Two-Arm Incline Dumbbell Chest Press** https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/two-arm-incline-dumbbell-chest-press
  > "Hold dumbbells at chest level with your elbows at approximately 45 degrees from your body."
  > "Lowering weights too quickly or bouncing at the bottom, which reduces time under tension and increases injury risk"
  > "Setting the bench too steep (above 45 degrees), which shifts emphasis to the anterior deltoid rather than the chest"
- **ACE, Incline Chest Press** https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/25/incline-chest-press/
  > "Inhale and slowly lower the dumbbells in unison and in a controlled movement towards your upper chest"
  > "Exhale and gently press upwards to full elbow extension"
  > "Keep the elbows under your wrists, and either close to your sides (emphasizes more triceps) or flared out from the body (emphasizes more pectorals)."
  > "Safety is very important in this exercise."
- **Stoppani, Master Chest Workouts**
  > "This is truly one of the best mass-builders you can do for the chest, particularly the upper pecs."
  > "The arc the dumbbells allow you to form as you press up and together targets more of the upper/inner pec fibers"

## 4. Reverse-Grip Incline Dumbbell Press
- **Stoppani, Master Chest Workouts** (seksioni "Reverse-Grip Incline Dumbbell Press")
  > "sit on an incline bench set to an angle of about 30-45 degrees."
  > "slowly lower the dumbbells to your lower chest and then reverse the motion"
  > "The dumbbells allow for a freer range of motion than a barbell"
  > "Be sure to use light weight until you get used to doing this exercise." (Stoppani e thotë për variantin me shufër; safety 1 e shënon kështu)
  > "turn your wrists to an overhand grip and finish the set with standard incline dumbbell presses"
- **Roy, Arseneault, Sercia 2021** https://journal.iusca.org/index.php/Journal/article/view/39 (DOI 10.47206/ijsc.v1i1.39)
  > "significantly higher at a close pronation at 0°and 30°; during a close supination at 30° and during a wide supination at 30°."
  > "Triceps brachii were better solicited during close pronation at 0° and -15° compared to supinated grip at 0° and 30°."
- Gabimet, kyçet dhe bërrylat: NASM dhe ACE të presës së pjerrët me shtanga (shih 3).

## 5. Incline Dumbbell Flye
- **ACE, Lying Chest Fly** https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/21/lying-chest-fly/ (ky burim është ushtrimi në stol të të sheshtë; nuk është burim për pjerrësinë dhe setup-i i Incline Dumbbell Flye nuk jep kënd stoli)
  > "Inhale and slowly lower the dumbbells in unison in a wide arc until they lie level with your shoulders or chest."
  > "Keep the dumbbells parallel with each other during the movement."
  > "Exhale and slowly raise the dumbbells upward in a wide arc back to your starting position"
  > "neutral wrist position (avoid flexion/extension at the wrist joint)"
  > "A spotter should adopt a split-stance position or half-kneeling position"
- **Stoppani, Master Chest Workouts**
  > "you're missing the tension where you need it most: At the top of the movement, when the hands come together."
  > "to completely isolate the upper pecs with a single-joint exercise."

## 6. Incline Cable Flye
- **ACE, Standing Incline Cable Flyes** https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/163/standing-incline-cable-flyes/ (versioni në këmbë; shih pyetjet)
  > "Position the cable handles level with the region between your knees and hips"
  > "do not allow your hands to move past the midline of your body (level of your armpit)"
  > "Exhale gently and slowly squeeze your chest muscles to pull your arms forward"
  > "Avoid the tendency to lean forward during this exercise as this increases the stresses within the shoulder joint"
- **Stoppani, Master Chest Workouts**
  > "Switching to cables, however, will provide the continuous tension you need to target the inner pec muscle fibers"

## 7. Dumbbell Flye
- **ACE, Lying Chest Fly** (shih 5)
  > "Your head, shoulders, butt and feet should make contact with the bench and floor/riser throughout the exercise."
- **Stoppani, Master Chest Workouts**
  > "a single-joint free weight movement performed on a flat bench that will isolate the pecs and hit the outer areas"
  > "Dumbbell flyes lead off Week 2 as a pre-exhaust before bench press."

## 8. Cable Crossover
- **NASM, Cable Crossover** https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/cable-crossover
  > "Set cable pulleys to approximately shoulder height."
  > "Pull both handles forward in a large arc, crossing them in front of your chest."
  > "Avoid letting the weight stack pull your arms back too quickly."
  > "Positioning pulleys too low, which changes the angle and reduces pectoral activation"
  > "Bringing the hands too close together at peak contraction, which can strain the shoulder joint"
- **Stoppani, Master Chest Workouts**
  > "In Week 3 you'll move the pulleys down low and pull the handles upward to target the upper pecs."
  > "This hits more of the middle portion of the chest."
  > "your arm crosses the midline of the body toward the opposite shoulder."

## 9. Close-Grip Bench Press
- **NASM, Close Grip Bench Press** https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/close-grip-bench-press
  > "Position your grip slightly narrower than shoulder-width apart."
  > "Avoid extremely narrow grips that compromise shoulder safety and increase wrist stress."
  > "Focus on triceps contraction throughout the pressing phase."
- **ACE, Close-grip Bench Press** https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/311/close-grip-bench-press/
  > "grip the bar with the hands directly in line with the shoulders and the elbows pointed towards the feet."
  > "keeping them close to the ribs."
- **Saeterbakken 2021** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8296276/
  > "wide grip displayed lower triceps brachii activation than medium and narrow."
  > "Both groups demonstrated lower 6-RM loads using a narrow grip compared to the other grips."
- **Stoppani, Master Chest Workouts**
  > "emphasizing triceps development and pressing strength while reducing chest involvement" (NASM, Close Grip Bench Press; tip i veçantë pranë Stoppanit)
  > "Another trick to use for hitting the inner pecs is to use a closer grip on bench pressing exercises."
