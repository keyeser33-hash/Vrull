Të gjitha burimet u hapën më 15.11.2026. Shenjat: [D] = thënë në dokument (me seksion), [S] = gjykimi im, [V] = vetëm ajo që kontrollova me mjet. Citatet janë në gjuhën e burimit. ## Tempo dhe fryma (të gjitha ushtrimet)
- Wilk, Zając, Tufano 2021, https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC8310485 (Key Points) [D]: "it seems that the most favorable is a combination of slower eccentric movements, paired with faster concentric movements"
- Androulakis Korakakis dhe kolegët 2023, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10801605/ (abstrakt) [D]: "employ a ROM that emphasizes training at long muscle lengths while also employing a repetition tempo between 2 and 8 s"
- Tempo {downS 2, upS 1} sipas rregullit të orkestruesit (downS = faza ekscentrike). Teksti thotë me fjalë "kthimi 2 s, tërheqja 1 s" [S].
- Fryma: ACE Seated Lat Pulldown: "Slowly exhale and initiate the downward pull by first depressing (lower) your scapulae". ACE Seated Row (makinë): "gently exhale and perform a pulling movement". ACE Single Arm Row: "Exhale and slowly pull the dumbbell upwards, bending your elbow and pulling your upper arm backwards."
- Pohimi "nxirre frymën kur tërheq" është [D] te ACE; "merre kur kthen" te rreshtat është [S] (ACE e thotë vetëm për Single Arm Row: "Inhale and gently lower the dumbbell to your starting position").

## Rreshti i dhimbjes (te safety e çdo ushtrimi)
- NASM, Corrective Exercise Red Flags, https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/corrective-exercise-red-flags [D, seksioni "Pain vs. Soreness" dhe "Sharp or Sudden"]: "Stop the exercise and decide whether continued training is right." dhe "responses that are sharp, escalate across sessions, or alter how a client moves"
- "ose pyet një specialist" është [S] nga fjalia e të njëjtit seksion: "warrant modification or referral".

## 1. Dumbbell Bent-Over Row
- setup 1–3 [D] Mayo, bent-over row with dumbbell, https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness/multimedia/bent-over-row/vid-20084680: "To do the bent-over row with a dumbbell, hold a dumbbell in your hand and stand with your feet comfortably apart." / "Bend your knees and lean forward at the hips, keeping your spine nice and straight." Dy shtanga në vend të një: [S]. "Gjerësia e shpatullave": Mayo e thotë në transkript "for most people, about shoulder-width" [V, WebFetch].
- setup 4 [D] ACE Single Arm Row, https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/126/single-arm-row/: "Depress and retract your scapulae (pull your shoulders down and back) without arching your low back"
- steps 1–2 [D] Stoppani, Back-Building Workout Breakdown, https://www.jimstoppani.com/training/back-building-workout-breakdown (seksioni "Supported Bent-Over Row"): "Pull the dumbbells up towards your hips, bringing your elbows up above your back as high as possible." Kujdes: teksti i tij përshkruan versionin me kraharor të mbështetur; tërheqja është e njëjta [S]. ACE: "Keep your arm close to the side of your body" dhe "pulling your upper arm backwards"; "Avoid rotating your body or changing the position of your spine throughout the lift."
- steps 3 [D] ACE: "Inhale and gently lower the dumbbell to your starting position while maintaining a flat back"
- (mistakes 1 me Fenwick u hoq: burimi nuk e thotë pasojën e rrumbullakosjes.)
- tip Fenwick 2009, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19620925/ [D]: "The standing bent-over row produced large activation symmetrically across the back but produced the largest lumbar spine load." (3 rreshta, 7 burra).
- mistakes 2, 3 [D] ACE: "without allowing your torso to rotate or shoulder to move towards the floor"; Mayo: "do not allow your shoulder to roll forward".
- mistakes 4 [D] Stoppani: "a lot of people have a hard time minimizing biceps involvement on pulling exercises like pulldowns and rows"
- (mistakes 5 dhe cue 5 me Wilk u hoqën: Wilk nuk e quan gabim uljen e shpejtë.)
- tip Stoppani [D]: "the dumbbells allow you to pull them higher in the top position of the row" dhe "This can help to stimulate even more muscle fibers in the lats, especially the lower lat muscle fibers." Stol i pjerrët: "set an incline bench so that the top of the bench is just below hip height"
- tip Fenwick [D]: "The inverted row elicited the highest activation of the latissimus dorsi muscles, upper back, and hip extensor muscles." dhe citati i mësipërm.
- tip Korakakis [D]: "emphasizes training at long muscle lengths" (pjesa për krahët e shtrirë u hoq).
- safety 1: pjesa "ule peshën" u hoq. safety 2 [D] Stoppani: "Not only will this exercise remove stress from the lower back, but it can help you to place more focus on the lats."

## 2. Seated Cable Row
Rishikim: ACE 168 (makinë me jastëk gjoksi) nuk përdoret më. Burimet: ACE 48 (kabllo) dhe përshkrimi i videos së NASM, lexuar nga orkestruesi.
- setup/steps/cues [D] ACE Seated Row (kabllo), https://www.acefitness.org/exerciselibrary/48/seated-row: "Use a seated pulley cable machine and a narrow handle, and place the feet on the floor with knees bent and maintain a straight back." / "Hold the handle and lift the chest while slowly pulling the elbows backwards close to the rib cage until the handle touches the front of the stomach." / "Pause for one second before slowly straightening the arms to return the weight to the original starting position."
- setup 3, steps 2, cues, mistakes 3 [D] NASM, "How to Do a Seated Machine Row: Close Grip" (përshkrimi i videos, lexuar nga orkestruesi), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0cTJCfxa0Y: "Keep a neutral spine, neck, and shoulders. - Brace the abs. - Pull the handle into the torso while also retracting the shoulder blades. - Return the weight under control."
- mistakes 1 [D] Stoppani, Back-Building Workout Breakdown, https://www.jimstoppani.com/training/back-building-workout-breakdown: "a lot of people have a hard time minimizing biceps involvement on pulling exercises like pulldowns and rows"
- mistakes 2 dhe 3 janë kundërtarët e udhëzimeve të mësipërme ("maintain a straight back", "Return the weight under control") pa pasojë të shtuar [S]; orkestruesi mund t'i heqë.
- breathing: vetëm tempo (Wilk, Korakakis, më sipër). Fryma e rreshtave u hoq (ishte nga ACE 168).
- tip Stoppani [D]: "Seated cable rows can be done with either a wide grip or narrow grip" dhe "adding in some one-arm work will encourage balanced development from side to side"; rripat: "your gripping muscles of the hands and forearms fatigue quicker than your back muscles"
- tip Lehman 2004, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC449729/ [D]: "Highest levels of myoelectric activity in the middle trapezius/rhomboid muscle group occurred during the seated row." / "Actively retracting the scapula did not influence middle trapezius/rhomboid activity."
- tip Di Fonza 2026, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13510307/ [D]: "a narrower grip and a lower shoulder-abduction angle were each associated with greater LD amplitude in separate studies"
- safety 1 [D] ACE 48: "maintain a straight back". Pasoja për belin u hoq.

## 3. Wide-Grip Pulldown
- setup 1 [D] NASM, The Biomechanics of the Lat Pulldown, https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/training/the-biomechanics-of-the-lat-pulldown-muscles-grip-and-form: "Set clients up with a tall posture, feet planted, and thighs secured beneath the pad." Stoppani: "sit with your butt way back on the pad" [D, seksioni "Stay Vertical"].
- setup 2 [D] Stoppani, Lat Pulldown, https://www.jimstoppani.com/training/lat-pulldown: "Grab a lat pulldown bar with an overhand grip outside of shoulder width"
- setup 3 [D] Stoppani: "you want to make sure your body is as vertical as possible with the line of pull of the movement"; ACE Seated Lat Pulldown, https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/158/seated-lat-pulldown/: "Lean back slightly (no more than a 30 degree angle), using your legs to support your body" Të dy shfaqen në tekst.
- setup 4 [D] Stoppani: "Before you start the pulldown movement, focus on squeezing your shoulder blades (scapulae) together and down."
- steps [D] NASM: "drive the elbows down and toward the ribs while bringing the bar toward the upper chest"; ACE: "Avoid any additional backwards lean during the pull movement."; ACE stop point: "or more importantly, you observe your elbows no longer moving downward, but now beginning to move backwards."
- mistakes 1 [D] Stoppani: "Essentially what you’re doing is making the movement more like a row"; 2–5 [D] NASM: "Pulling primarily with the arms." / "Shrugged shoulders throughout the pull." / "Excessive leaning or swinging." / "Forward head posture as fatigue sets in."
- mistakes 6 / safety 1 [D] ACE (citati i stop point) dhe "Avoid any further pulling as this places additiomnal stresses into the shoulder joint." (shkrimi si te burimi).
- tip Stoppani [D]: "the elbows flare out, and this directs the focus up high in the lats"; "focus on pulling it down to your collarbones"; "Taking an open grip helps reduce that squeeze from your hands."; "wrist straps are also very helpful with this exercise for overloading the lats". (Pohimi "bicepsi shtrëngon më pak" u hoq.)
- tip Andersen 2014, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24662157/ [D]: "In the eccentric phase, there was greater activation using wide vs. narrow grip for latissimus and infraspinatus (p ≤ 0.04)";  "The 6RM strengths with narrow (80.3 ± 7.2 kg) and medium grip (80 ± 7.1 kg) were higher than wide grip" / "athletes and others engaged in resistance training can generally expect similar muscle activation"
- tip Lusk 2010, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20543740/ [D]: "a pronated grip elicited greater LD activity than a supinated grip (p < 0.05), but had no influence of grip type on the MT and BB muscles"
- tip NASM [D]: "Often feels more challenging, especially for beginners." / "Balanced option for most clients."
- safety 2 [D] NASM: "One variation that's generally best avoided is the behind-the-neck pulldown."

## 4. Behind-Neck Pulldown
Burimet nuk bien dakord (shih raportin): NASM [seksioni "Common Compensations" dhe FAQ] "For most clients, no. Front pulldowns effectively train the same muscles while placing less stress on the shoulders and cervical spine." dhe "One variation that's generally best avoided is the behind-the-neck pulldown."; Sperandei 2009, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19855327/ (abstrakt): "BNL is not a good lat pull-down technique and should be avoided." dhe "For LD, there was no difference between techniques."; Stoppani [seksioni "Behind-the-Neck Pulldowns"]: "Not if you do it correctly!"
- setup/steps/cues/mistakes [D] Stoppani: "So what you want to do is face away from the machine for the behind-the-neck pulldown." / "you can pull a bench over and place it in front of the seat to give you more room to move forward" / "Dropping your chin like this is the LAST thing you want to do – NOT good for the cervical spine." / "Stop at about eye level (behind your head, of course), no further down." / "the lower I go, the more external rotation I’m getting at the shoulders" / "You also want to take a very wide grip on the bar"
- tip Stoppani [D]: "a lot bodybuilders swear by the behind-the-neck pulldown for maximizing lat width"
- tip Sperandei: citatet e mësipërme; gjoksi, deltoidi i pasmë dhe bicepsi kishin diferenca [D] nga abstrakti (PM, PD, BB kishin diferenca).
- safety 2 [D] Di Fonza 2026 (seksioni 3.1): "in the absence of direct injury-outcome data, this should be framed as a biomechanical caution rather than a demonstrated safety difference"
- safety 3 [D] NASM Red Flags: "Stop training and recommend medical evaluation." (vetëm për mpirje/gudulisje); dhimbja e mprehtë ka rreshtin e saj me "Stop the exercise and decide whether continued training is right." Safety 1: "For most clients, no. Front pulldowns effectively train the same muscles while placing less stress on the shoulders and cervical spine." "Kalo te Wide-Grip" u hoq. Steps 3 u hoq.
- Hequr: pohimi i Stoppani se EMG tregon më shumë fibra të sipërme të lats (nuk përputhet me Sperandei: LD pa diferencë); nuk hyri.

## 5. Reverse-Grip Pulldown
- setup/steps [D] Stoppani: "The reverse-grip is just flipping the hands around so that your palms face in toward you, but the elbow motion is still the same"; "the elbows move to the front of the body and go from front to back as you pull down"; ACE, NASM si te Wide-Grip.
- mistakes 1 [D] NASM: "Increases biceps involvement."; Stoppani: "that’s why the biceps take over"
- tip Stoppani [D]: "the elbows stay in front of the body instead of flaring out. This directs more focus onto the lower portion of the lats."; "it's a bit of a stronger pulldown because you have more biceps involvement, so you can use more weight"; "you should do a fair variety of all major versions: wide, narrow (close), and reverse"
- tip Lusk [D]: citati i mësipërm. tip Lehman [D]: "The wide grip lat pulldown had a siginificantly higher ratio than the reverse grip lat pulldown" (raport LD/biceps; kapje izometrike 10 s)
- tip NASM [D]: "Typically allows clients to lift more weight." / "Useful for clients working toward chin-ups."
- Mospërputhje: Stoppani thotë se kapja e kundërt synon lats e poshtme; Lusk dhe Lehman dhanë më pak aktivitet të lats. Të dyja janë në tekst.

## 6. Standing Pulldown
- setup [D] ACE Seated Lat Pulldown, https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/158/seated-lat-pulldown/ (variacioni në këmbë): "Maintain a slight bend in your knees and shift your weight back onto your rear leg to create the backward lean." / "Avoid flexing (bending) forward at the hips during the pulling movement." (qëndrimi me këmbë në pozicion ecjeje: fjalia e parë e variacionit, me një shkronjë të gabuar në burim.)
- setup 3, steps, cues, mistakes [D] Jim Stoppani, "Standing Pulldown: A Great Hybrid Exercise" (përshkrimi i videos, lexuar nga orkestruesi), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HynaSdVSKMw: "Use a wide grip on the bar, and pull it down in front of you to chest level, bending at the elbows and rotating your shoulders. Hold it there for a second or two, then slowly control the bar as it returns to starting position."
- tip [D] i njëjti përshkrim: "A perfect hybrid exercise between the wide grip lat pulldown and the seated row" / "The standing version of the pulldown is a compound exercise that requires motion in both the shoulders and elbows." / "The standing version focuses more resistance from the weight on your lats."
- mistakes 2 dhe 3 janë kundërtarë të udhëzimeve ("slowly control the bar", "Use a wide grip") [S]; mistakes 1 është "Avoid flexing" [D] ACE. Orkestruesi mund të heqë 2–3, por atëherë ushtrimi ka vetëm 1 gabim dhe bie nën minimum.
- safety [D] ACE: "This exercise targets the major back muscles effectively, but should be performed without compromise to the shoulder or low back." (Fryma, sinjalet dhe gabimet e huazuara nga pulldown-i i ulur u hoqën.)
