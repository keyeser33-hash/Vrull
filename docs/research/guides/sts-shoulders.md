## Dumbbell Shoulder Press


### setup

1. Ulu në stol me mbështetëse, me kokën, shpatullat dhe vithet në stol dhe këmbët të sheshta në dysheme. Në stol të rregullueshëm vendose në 90°.  /  Sit on a bench with a back rest, with your head, shoulders and glutes against the bench and your feet flat on the floor. On an adjustable bench, set it to 90°.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 1: "so that your head, shoulders and butt make contact with the bench."
   - [D] ace_press › Step 1: "Place your feet firmly on the floor."
   - [D] st_press › Bench angle (seated): "If using an adjustable bench, have it set to a 90-degree angle, not leaned back."
2. Forco barkun, tërhiq shpatullat poshtë dhe pas dhe mbaji kështu gjatë gjithë setit.  /  Brace your core, pull your shoulder blades down and back and hold that position for the whole set.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 1: "Stiffen your torso by contracting your core and abdominal muscles"
   - [D] ace_press › Step 1: "Retract and depress your scapulae (pull down and back)"
3. Ngri hallterat te shpatullat, pëllëmbët përpara, në gjerësinë e shpatullave ose pak më gjerë, me kyçet drejt.  /  Bring the dumbbells to shoulder level, palms facing forward, at shoulder width or slightly wider, with your wrists straight.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 1: "with a closed, pronated grip (palms facing forward and thumbs wrapped around the handles)."
   - [D] ace_press › Step 1: "at shoulder-width or slightly wider and wrists in a neutral position"
4. Mbaji bërrylat pak përpara trupit, jo drejt në vijën e shpatullave.  /  Keep your elbows slightly in front of your torso, not in line with your shoulders.
   - [D] st_press › Elbow position: "they're still slightly in front of the shoulders and torso, not directly in line with them."
   - [D] ace_press › Step 2: "For more comfort through the range-of-motion keep your elbows pointed in front of you."

### steps

1. Nxirre ajrin dhe shtyj të dyja hallterat lart mbi kokë njëkohësisht, pa harkuar belin.  /  Breathe out and press both dumbbells overhead together, without arching your lower back.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 2: "press the dumbbells overhead in unison until your elbows are fully extended"
   - [D] ace_press › Step 2: "without any arching in your low back."
2. Në krye ndalo kur bërrylat janë pothuajse drejt, pa i bllokuar.  /  At the top, stop when your elbows are almost straight, without locking them.
   - [D] st_press › Range of motion: "Stop just shy of full elbow lockout at the top"
3. Ule hallterat ngadalë duke përkulur bërrylat, deri poshtë nivelit të mjekrës.  /  Lower the dumbbells slowly by bending your elbows, to below chin level.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 3: "in a controlled manner by flexing (bending) your elbows"
   - [D] st_press › Range of motion: "When you come down, lower the bar or the handles of the dumbbells to below chin level."
4. Mos e lër peshën të bjerë vetë: tërhiqe poshtë me kontroll, duke përdorur shpinën e gjerë.  /  Don't let the weight simply drop: pull it down under control, using your lats.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 3: "Avoid simply yielding to gravity,"
   - [D] ace_press › Step 3: "consciously try to contract your lats and pull the dumbbells down towards you."

### breathing

1. Poshtë 2 s, lart 1 s: Wilk dhe kolegët e quajnë më të favorshme uljen më të ngadaltë (ekscentrike) me ngritje më të shpejtë (koncentrike); Androulakis Korakakis dhe kolegët thonë se nuk është e qartë cila fazë e zgjatur jep më shumë rritje.  /  Down 2 s, up 1 s: Wilk and colleagues find a slower lowering (eccentric) with a faster lift (concentric) most favourable; Androulakis Korakakis and colleagues say it is unclear which lengthened phase gives more growth.
   - [D] wilk › Key Points: "it seems that the most favorable is a combination of slower eccentric movements, paired with faster concentric movements"
   - [D] korakakis › Discussion (cited by the coordinator, not re-opened) ≈: "unclear whether extending the concentric or eccentric phase… will lead to greater hypertrophy"
2. Një përsëritje zgjat nga 2 deri në 8 s; si e ndan mes uljes dhe ngritjes është zgjedhja jote.  /  A rep lasts 2 to 8 s; how you split it between lowering and lifting is up to you.
   - [D] korakakis › Abstract: "employ a repetition tempo between 2 and 8 s"
3. Nxirre ajrin kur shtyn hallterat lart, merr frymë kur i ul.  /  Breathe out as you press the dumbbells up, breathe in as you lower them.
   - [D] ace_press › Steps 2-3 ≈: "Exhale and press the dumbbells overhead; Inhale and lower the dumbbells"

### cues

1. Shpina në stol, pa harkuar belin  /  Back on the bench, don't arch
   - [D] ace_press › Step 2: "without any arching in your low back."
2. Shpatullat poshtë dhe pas  /  Shoulders down and back
   - [D] ace_press › Step 1: "Retract and depress your scapulae (pull down and back)"
3. Bërrylat pak përpara trupit  /  Elbows slightly in front
   - [D] st_press › Elbow position: "they're still slightly in front of the shoulders and torso, not directly in line with them."
4. Ule poshtë nivelit të mjekrës  /  Lower to below chin level
   - [D] st_press › Range of motion: "When you come down, lower the bar or the handles of the dumbbells to below chin level."
5. Mos i blloko bërrylat lart  /  Don't lock your elbows out
   - [D] st_press › Range of motion: "Stop just shy of full elbow lockout at the top"

### mistakes

1. Harkimi i belit për ta shtyrë peshën lart: shpina ndahet nga stoli dhe ngarkesa shkon te beli.  /  Arching your lower back to push the weight up: your back leaves the bench and the load shifts to your lower back.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 2: "without any arching in your low back."
   - [D] ace_press › Step 2: "Maintain your body contact with the bench, neutral wrist (avoid flexion and extension) and depressed, retracted scapulae positions."
2. Bllokimi i plotë i bërrylave në krye: Stoppani e quan të pasigurt për nyjet.  /  Locking the elbows fully at the top: Stoppani calls it unsafe for your joints.
   - [D] st_press › Range of motion: "Locking out the elbows at the top is not only unsafe for your joints"
3. Kyçet e përkulur përpara ose prapa gjatë shtytjes.  /  Bending your wrists forwards or backwards during the press.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 1: "avoid flexion and extension of the wrists"
4. Bërrylat drejt anash, jo pak përpara: ACE i këshillon përpara për më shumë rehati gjatë gjithë lëvizjes.  /  Elbows straight out to the sides rather than slightly forward: ACE advises forward for more comfort through the whole range.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 2: "For more comfort through the range-of-motion keep your elbows pointed in front of you."
5. Ulja si lëshim i peshës nga graviteti, pa kontroll.  /  Lowering as if letting gravity take the weight, with no control.
   - [D] ace_press › Step 3: "Avoid simply yielding to gravity,"

### tips

1. **Jim Stoppani, PhD · autori i Shortcut to Size (How To Do A Shoulder Press)** — Kap hallterat me kapje të hapur, me gishtin e madh në të njëjtën anë me gishtat: dorezat ulen më poshtë në pëllëmbë, larg nyjeve të gishtave. Nëse të frikëson rrëshqitja, përdor rripa për kyçin.  /  Use an open grip, with your thumb on the same side as your fingers: the handles sit lower in your palm, away from your knuckles. If you worry about them slipping, use wrist straps.
   - [D] st_press › Grip: "the open grip keeps the bar/handle lower in the palm, down by the meat of the hand versus up near the knuckles."
2. **Jim Stoppani, PhD · autori i Shortcut to Size (How To Do A Shoulder Press)** — Kur bërrylat dalin anash, punon më shumë deltoidi i mesit; kur krahët e sipërm janë përpara trupit, deltoidi i përparmë. Për deltoidin e mesit mbaje busin vertikal.  /  When your elbows flare out, the middle delt works more; when your upper arms are in front of your body, the front delt does. For the middle delt, keep your torso vertical.
   - [D] st_press › Elbow position: "When the elbows come out to the sides, it's hitting more middle delt"
   - [D] st_press › Elbow position: "when the upper arms are in front of the body, it's emphasizing the front delts more"
   - [D] st_press › Safety: "if it's shoulder day and the goal is to hit the middle delts, keep your torso vertical."
3. **ACE, Dynamite Delts · Sweeney, Porcari dhe kolegët, UW-La Crosse** — Në studimin e ACE me University of Wisconsin–La Crosse, shtytja me halltera dha aktivizimin më të lartë të deltoidit të përparmë nga të gjitha ushtrimet e testuara.  /  In ACE's study with the University of Wisconsin–La Crosse, the dumbbell shoulder press produced the highest front delt activation of all the exercises tested.
   - [D] ace_delts › Findings › Anterior deltoid ≈: "elicited significantly higher muscle activation than any other exercise tested."
4. **Androulakis Korakakis dhe kolegët, 2023 · Journal of Functional Morphology and Kinesiology** — Rishikimi rekomandon një lëvizje që e shtrin muskulin plotësisht.  /  The review recommends a range of motion that fully stretches the muscle.
   - [D] korakakis › Practical recommendations: "Employ a ROM that allows for muscles to be fully stretched."

### safety

1. Me pesha të rënda kërko një ndihmës: ACE thotë që roja është e rëndësishme dhe qëndron pas teje.  /  With heavy loads ask for a spotter: ACE says spotting is important, and the spotter stands behind you.
   - [D] ace_press › Spotting (Caution): "Spotting is important to ensure safety in this exercise."
   - [D] ace_press › Spotting (Caution): "The spotter should stand behind you in a split-stance position"
2. Shmang pozicionin e skajshëm 'high-five' në fund të lëvizjes dhe zgjidh shtytjen para trupit, jo prapa kokës.  /  Avoid the end-range 'high-five' position and choose pressing in front of your body, not behind your head.
   - [D] kolber2010 › Practical Applications: "avoiding the end-range 'high-five' position through exercise modifications (Figures 7A, B) may prevent the development of anterior shoulder instability and pain."
3. Mann San Gil, trajner i çertifikuar nga ACE, thotë se me probleme aktuale të shpatullës mund të duhet të shmangen shtytjet mbi kokë; si alternativë sugjeron ngritje anash ose përpara.  /  Mann San Gil, an ACE Certified Personal Trainer, says people with current shoulder problems may want to avoid overhead presses; he suggests lateral or front raises as alternatives.
   - [D] ace_smart › Expert advice: "People with current shoulder issues may want to avoid doing overhead presses."
4. Dhimbje e mprehtë ose e papritur: ndalo ushtrimin; kur vazhdon ose shtohet, flit me një specialist.  /  Sharp or sudden pain: stop the exercise; if it persists or gets worse, see a professional.
   - [D] nasm_red › Exercise Red Flags › Sharp or Sudden: "Sharp or sudden pain is a signal to stop exercising and decide whether continued training is right."
   - [D] nasm_red › Referring out: "Refer when signs persist, escalate, or fall outside of professional scope."

## Dumbbell Lateral Raise


### setup

1. Qëndro drejt, me këmbët pak më gjerë se ijët ose në pozicion të ndarë, me hallterat anash kofshëve dhe pëllëmbët nga trupi.  /  Stand tall with your feet slightly wider than your hips or in a split stance, with the dumbbells alongside your thighs and your palms facing your body.
   - [D] ace_lat › Step 1 (starting position): "Position the dumbbells alongside your thighs with your elbows extended or holding a slight bend."
2. Forco barkun, tërhiq shpatullat poshtë dhe pas dhe mbaje kokën në vijë me shpinën.  /  Brace your core, pull your shoulder blades down and back and keep your head in line with your spine.
   - [D] ace_lat › Step 2 (set your torso) ≈: "Brace your abdominal and core muscles"
   - [D] ace_lat › Step 2 (set your torso): "depress and retract your scapulae"
3. Mbaji bërrylat drejt ose me përkulje të lehtë gjatë gjithë setit.  /  Keep your elbows straight or slightly bent for the whole set.
   - [D] ace_lat › Step 1 (starting position): "Position the dumbbells alongside your thighs with your elbows extended or holding a slight bend."

### steps

1. Nxirre ajrin dhe ngri hallterat lart e anash, me bërrylat dhe krahët e sipërm që prijnë pak para parakrahëve.  /  Breathe out and raise the dumbbells up and out to your sides, with your elbows and upper arms leading slightly ahead of your forearms.
   - [D] ace_lat › Upward phase ≈: "Exhale and raise the dumbbells up and out to your sides"
   - [D] ace_lat › Upward phase ≈: "Your elbows and upper arms should lead slightly ahead of your forearms and dumbbells"
2. Ngri deri sa krahët të jenë afërsisht paralel me dyshemenë dhe mos kalo 90°.  /  Raise until your arms are roughly parallel to the floor and don't go beyond 90°.
   - [D] st_shoulders › Leaning dumbbell lateral raises: "For each rep, raise your arm until it's parallel with the floor."
   - [D] kolber2014 › Abstract (conclusion): "Avoiding performance of lateral deltoid raises and upright rows beyond an angle of 90°"
3. Ule hallterat ngadalë, me bërrylat pak të përkulur.  /  Lower the dumbbells slowly, with your elbows slightly bent.
   - [D] ace_lat › Downward phase ≈: "Inhale and lower the dumbbells slowly, keeping your elbows slightly extended"
4. Mbaje busin drejt dhe kyçet neutrale gjatë gjithë lëvizjes.  /  Keep your torso upright and your wrists neutral throughout.
   - [D] ace_lat › Tips: "Maintain your torso erect (no arching your low back)"
   - [D] ace_lat › Tips ≈: "Keep your wrists neutral and avoid bending them up or down"

### breathing

1. Poshtë 2 s, lart 1 s: Wilk dhe kolegët e quajnë më të favorshme uljen më të ngadaltë (ekscentrike) me ngritje më të shpejtë (koncentrike); Androulakis Korakakis dhe kolegët thonë se nuk është e qartë cila fazë e zgjatur jep më shumë rritje.  /  Down 2 s, up 1 s: Wilk and colleagues find a slower lowering (eccentric) with a faster lift (concentric) most favourable; Androulakis Korakakis and colleagues say it is unclear which lengthened phase gives more growth.
   - [D] wilk › Key Points: "it seems that the most favorable is a combination of slower eccentric movements, paired with faster concentric movements"
   - [D] korakakis › Discussion (cited by the coordinator, not re-opened) ≈: "unclear whether extending the concentric or eccentric phase… will lead to greater hypertrophy"
2. Një përsëritje zgjat nga 2 deri në 8 s; si e ndan mes uljes dhe ngritjes është zgjedhja jote.  /  A rep lasts 2 to 8 s; how you split it between lowering and lifting is up to you.
   - [D] korakakis › Abstract: "employ a repetition tempo between 2 and 8 s"
3. Nxirre ajrin kur i ngre hallterat anash, merr frymë kur i ul.  /  Breathe out as you raise the dumbbells to the sides, breathe in as you lower them.
   - [D] ace_lat › Upward phase ≈: "Exhale and raise the dumbbells up and out to your sides"
   - [D] ace_lat › Downward phase ≈: "Inhale and lower the dumbbells slowly, keeping your elbows slightly extended"

### cues

1. Ngri deri paralel me dyshemenë  /  Raise to parallel with the floor
   - [D] st_shoulders › Leaning dumbbell lateral raises: "For each rep, raise your arm until it's parallel with the floor."
2. Bërrylat prijnë, jo duart  /  Elbows lead, not your hands
   - [D] ace_lat › Upward phase ≈: "Your elbows and upper arms should lead slightly ahead of your forearms and dumbbells"
3. Trupi drejt, pa lëkundje  /  Torso upright, no swinging
