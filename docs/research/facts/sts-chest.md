## bench-press-1
- Pohimi: në garat e para shufra shpesh sillej me pullover nga dyshemeja ose i jepej lifterit; stoli i York (1953, 29,95 dollarë); stoli i Marilyn Monroe 1952 pa mbajtëse.
- Lidhja: https://www.starkcenter.org/igh/igh-v1/igh-v1-n1/igh0101i.pdf (Roark, Iron Game History, vol. 1 nr. 1, shkurt 1990)
- Fjalia (nga teksti i PDF): "often involved either a pullover from the floor to starting position, or the weight was handed to the lifter."
- Fjalia 2: "The bench by the York Barbell Company was introduced by Jim Park in the February 1953 issue of Strength & Health magazine"; "the ad on page 37 reveals the price at $29.95."
- Fjalia 3: "The bench Marilyn Monroe used in 1952 was flat, unadjustable, unpadded, and had no uprights attached to hold a barbell"
- Shënim: "Roark pyet kur u shtuan mbajtëset" = "When did uprights become attached to benches?" (e njëjta kolonë). Floyd Page nuk përsëritet (është te udhëzuesi).

## bench-press-2, incline-dumbbell-flye-1, cable-crossover-1 (e njëjta kërkim ACE)
- Lidhja: https://www.acefitness.org/certifiednewsarticle/2884 (ACE Certified News, tetor 2012; Schanke M.S., Porcari Ph.D., Felix Ph.D., Hendrix M.S.Ed., Foster Ph.D., UW-La Crosse; i financuar vetëm nga ACE). Teksti i faqes me curl.
- bench-press-2: "researchers found that out of the nine exercises tested, the pectoralis major was activated the most during the barbell bench press." 14 burra 19–30 vjeç; 80% e 1RM-së; pesë përsëritje; "The exercises that elicited the least amount of activation were actually the push-ups" (61–63%, "we didn't add any weight to the push-ups").
- incline-dumbbell-flye-1: Tabela 1: "Inclined Dumbbell Flys 69 ± 30.5*"; "*Significantly lower than barbell bench press (p < .05)". Abstrakti i tezës (UW-La Crosse, Schanke 2012, WebFetch, përmbledhje): bench press, pec deck dhe bent-forward cable crossover pa dallim të rëndësishëm.
- cable-crossover-1: "Both the pec deck (at 98% of muscle activation compared to barbell bench press) and the bent-forward cable crossover (93% of muscle activation) elicited nearly equivalent muscle activation as the barbell bench press." Tabela: crossover "93 ± 22.0" pa yll, RPE "5.1 ± 1.60" kundrejt "6.5 ± 1.98". Porcari, Ph.D.: "you can use the barbell bench press, pec deck or the cable crossovers interchangeably". Titulli i Porcarit: te vetë artikulli ("John P. Porcari, Ph.D."), UW-La Crosse.
- Kufizim: ushtrimi i testuar është "bent-forward cable crossover" (formulimi i faktit e ruan). Këndi i stolit te flyes së pjerrëta nuk jepet te artikulli.

## incline-bench-press-1
- Lidhja: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7449336/ (Chaves dhe kolegët, Int J Exerc Sci 2020;13(6):859-872). Abstrakti nga Europe PMC.
- Fjalia: "The changes in the second intercostal space of the pectoralis major was greatest in the incline pressure group compared with the horizontal [mean difference (95% CI) of 0.62 (0.23, 1.0) cm, p=0.003]"
- Fjalia: "Forty-seven untrained men"; "Training was conducted once a week for eight weeks"; "There was no difference between groups for the change in horizontal bench press isometric strength (~ 10 kg increase, p=0.776) or incline bench press isometric strength (~ 11 kg increase, p=0.333)"; "Changes in muscle thickness differed only in one of the three sites."
- 44°: "The incline bench press exercise was performed at 44 degrees above horizontal." (WebFetch i faqes PMC, përmbledhje; faqja HTML ktheu reCAPTCHA te curl).

## incline-dumbbell-press-1
- Lidhja: https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2010.543916 (Saeterbakken, van den Tillaar, Fimland; J Sports Sci 2011;29(5):533-538). Abstrakti nga Europe PMC.
- Fjalia: "The dumbbell load was 14% less than that for the Smith machine" ; "and 17% less than that for the barbell"; "Electrical activity in the pectoralis major and anterior deltoid did not differ during the lifts."; "Electrical activity in the biceps brachii increased with stability requirements"; "triceps brachii activity was reduced using dumbbells versus barbell".
- Kufizim i hapur: abstrakti nuk thotë këndin e stolit; teksti i plotë nuk u hap (T&F bllokoi). Fakti thotë "presa e gjoksit", jo "stol i pjerrët".

## reverse-grip-incline-dumbbell-press-1
- Lidhja: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16095407/ (Lehman GJ, J Strength Cond Res 2005;19(3):587-591). Abstrakti nga Europe PMC.
- Fjalia: "A supinated grip resulted in increased activity for the biceps brachii and the clavicular portion of the pectoralis major."
- Fjalia: "12 healthy men ... during an isometric hold of 5 different bench press exercises."
- Kufizim: stol i sheshtë, shufër, izometrike; thuhet te "Më shumë".

## dumbbell-flye-1
- Lidhja: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7675616/ (Solstad dhe kolegët, J Sports Sci Med 2020;19(4):645-651). Teksti i plotë nga PDF (jssm.org/19-4-645.p_d_f), pdftotext.
- Fjalia: "The total 6RM loads in the BBP and the DF was 88.5 ± 16.0kg and 40.5 ± 7.7kg"
- Fjalia: "Mean RMS EMG activity in the biceps brachii of the whole movement was higher in the DF (76%, p < 0.001, ES = 3.07) compared to the BBP"
- Fjalia (abstrakt): "higher muscle activation in the whole movement and the majority of the lifting phases for pectoralis major, deltoids anterior, and triceps brachii for the BBP compared to the DF (8-81 %, p ≤ 0.05)"; "both exercises could be included in training programs, but the BBP should be emphasized because of the higher muscle activation overall."
- Shënim: "pesha totale" = fjala e burimit "total 6RM loads"; PDF nuk e thotë shprehimisht nëse përfshin shufrën.

## close-grip-bench-press-1
- Lidhja: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8296276/ (Saeterbakken dhe kolegët, IJERPH 2021;18(12):6444). Teksti i plotë nga Europe PMC XML.
- Fjalia: "Post hoc analysis displayed 7.0% and 7.1% lower 6-RM loads with a narrow grip compared to both medium ( p < 0.001, ES = 0.55) and wide grips"
- Fjalia: "For the NT group, 8.4% and 6.6% lower 6-RM loads were observed using the narrow grip compared to medium ... and wide grips"
- Fjalia: "The distance between the arm pits was measured and defined as narrow grip (i.e., distance between index fingers)."
- Fjalia: "Twenty-eight men ... resistance-trained group (RT; n = 15) or the novice-trained group (NT; n = 13)"; abstrakt: "wide grip displayed lower triceps brachii activation than medium and narrow."

## Ndreqjet pas kontrollit të pavarur
- bench-press-2: teksti tani thotë se pec deck (98%) dhe cable crossover (93%) nuk dalloheshin statistikisht nga bench press-i; shtatë ushtrimet e tjera ("*Significantly lower than barbell bench press (p < .05)") dolën më të ulëta.
- incline-dumbbell-press-1: teksti thotë qartë se abstrakti nuk thotë që stoli ishte i pjerrët.
- reverse-grip-incline-dumbbell-press-1: teksti thotë "bench press me shufër në stol të sheshtë" (Lehman: isometric hold, flat bench press).
- incline-dumbbell-flye-1: hoqa "vetëm" (69%).
