# sts-biceps: faktet dhe provat (kontrolluar 2026-11-16)

Shënim: abstraktet vijnë nga Europe PMC (REST, fjalë për fjalë). Fjalitë e ACE-s janë citate që ktheu WebFetch (përmbledhje e WebFetch). PMC/NCBI Bookshelf ktheu reCAPTCHA, ndaj StatPearls u lexua nga abstrakti te Europe PMC (ID 30137823).

## barbell-curl-1
- Pohimi: me pëllëmbët lart EMG i bicepsit gjatë ngritjes: +19% ndaj pronates, +12% ndaj neutrales; brachioradialis +5% / +6%; shpatulla e përparme më pak. 10 kulturistë garues, sete 6 përsëritjesh me 8RM.
- Lidhja: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10054060/ (Coratella dhe kolegët, 2023, Sports, doi 10.3390/sports11030064)
- Provë (abstrakt, Europe PMC): "biceps brachii excitation was greater with the supinated compared to the pronated [+19(7)%, ES: 2.60] and neutral handgrip [+12(9)%, ES: 1.24]"; "Ten competitive bodybuilders performed bilateral biceps curl in non-exhaustive 6-rep sets using 8-RM"; "the brachioradialis showed greater excitation with the supinated compared to the pronated [+5(4)%...] and neutral handgrip [+6(5)%...]"; "anterior deltoid excitation was greater with the pronated and neutral handgrip compared to the supinated".
- Shënim: "+19%" është siç del te burimi (nRMS i normalizuar), nuk e kam shndërruar në përqindje relative.

## preacher-curl-1 dhe dumbbell-incline-curl-1 (i njëjti studim, dy pohime të ndryshme)
- Lidhja: https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2517-0509 (Kassiano dhe kolegët, 2025, Int J Sports Med)
- Provë (abstrakt, Europe PMC): "Sixty-three young women performed the preacher biceps curl (n=30) or the incline biceps curl (n=33) for 8 weeks, twice a week."
- preacher-curl-1: "The preacher biceps curl showed a greater increase in three repetition maximum tests in the preacher curl (mean difference=1.88 kg [95%CI: 1.14, 2.62 kg]). The incline biceps curl showed a greater increase in three repetition maximum tests in the incline biceps curl (mean difference=0.86 kg [95%CI: 0.10, 1.62 kg])."; "Strength gains appear to follow the principle of specificity."
- dumbbell-incline-curl-1: "greater increase in the proximal elbow flexor thickness in the incline biceps curl ... (mean difference=0.08 cm...)"; "greater increase in the distal elbow flexor thickness in the preacher biceps curl ... (mean difference=0.10 cm...)"; "suggest regional differences in muscle growth".
- Shënim: "afër shpatullës/afër bërrylit" = proximal/distal; "0,8 dhe 1 mm" është shndërrimi im nga 0,08 dhe 0,10 cm. Abstrakti nuk thotë "dumbbell"; teksti nuk e përmend.

## preacher-curl-2
- Pohimi: 19 gra të patrajnuara, 8 javë, preacher curl me dumbbell, 0°–68° kundër 68°–135°: CSA më e madhe në 70% të gjatësisë së bicepsit dhe 1RM më i madh me 0°–68°.
- Lidhja: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9960616 (Pedrosa dhe kolegët, 2023, Sports, doi 10.3390/sports11020039)
- Provë (abstrakt): "Nineteen young women had one arm randomly assigned to train in the initial ROM (INITIAL ROM: 0°-68°; 0° = extended elbow) while the contralateral arm trained in the final ROM (FINAL ROM: 68°-135°), three times per week over an eight-week study period."; "The INITIAL ROM protocol displayed a greater CSA increase than FINAL ROM protocol at 70% of biceps length (p = 0.001)"; "similar increases between the protocols for CSA at 50% (p = 0.311)"; "INITIAL ROM displayed a greater 1RM increase than FINAL ROM (p < 0.001)".
- Shënim: "seated dumbbell preacher curl" sipas objektivit.

## incline-cable-curl-1
- Pohimi: 15 burra, 10 javë, kabllo; biceps 6–9%, brachialis 8–10% (preacher 6/7/7% dhe 10%; Bayesian 9/9/9% dhe 8%); pa dallim të rëndësishëm mes dy formave dhe mes muskujve.
- Lidhja: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11906226/ (Attarieh dhe kolegët, 2025, Eur J Sport Sci, doi 10.1002/ejsc.12279)
- Provë (abstrakt + tekst i plotë, Europe PMC): "Both conditions showed significant increases in muscle thickness (p < 0.05) with no significant differences between them (p > 0.05) across the biceps brachii proximal, mid, and distal regions (... PREA: 6%[0.51], 7%[0.49], 7%[0.53]; BAYE: 9%[0.73], 9%[0.62], 9%[0.62]) and brachialis (PREA: 10%[0.72]; BAYE: 8%[0.65])"; "no significant difference was detected between biceps brachii and brachialis for PREA ... or BAYE"; "twice a week for 10 weeks (3-5 sets, 8-12RM)".
- Shënim: "krahu pas trupit" = shpatulla në ekstensionim (~50°), sipas burimit.

## one-arm-high-cable-curl-1 (fakt i përgjithshëm anatomik; nuk gjeta burim të pranuar specifik për high cable curl)
- Pohimi: dy koka në lidhjen e sipërme; kalon mbi bërryl; supinatori më i fuqishëm i parakrahut kur bërryli është i përkulur; popeye deformity.
- Lidhja: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK519538/ (Tiwana MS, Charlick M, Varacallo MA, StatPearls, 2024)
- Provë (abstrakt, Europe PMC): "this muscle's proximal attachment has 2 heads. The short head is sometimes referred to as 'caput breve,' while the long head is also called 'caput longum.'"; "Distally, the biceps brachii continues as the bicipital aponeurosis, crossing the elbow joint"; "becomes the forearm's most powerful supinator when flexed"; "'popeye deformity' ... arises from a ruptured long head tendon ... the muscle forms a ball at the anterior mid-arm."
- Titulli i autorëve: nuk e verifikova (burimi është libër i NIH/NCBI Bookshelf, jo ekspert individual). "supinim = pëllëmbë lart" është përkufizim i përgjithshëm, jo nga abstrakti.

## behind-back-cable-curl-1
- Pohimi: 30 burra të patrajnuar (24 e mbaruan), një krah neutral dhe një me shpatullën në ekstensionim maksimal, 10 javë: trashësia +7–9% në të dyja, pa dallim kuptimplotë.
- Lidhja: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13076143/ (Larsen dhe kolegët, 2026, Frontiers in Physiology, doi 10.3389/fphys.2026.1750722)
- Provë (abstrakt): "Thirty untrained men participated, with one arm assigned to train at the individualized peak shoulder extension angle and the contralateral arm at a neutral position. Twenty-four participants completed the intervention"; "Both conditions produced increases in muscle thickness of approximately 7%-9%."; "altering shoulder extension angle in isolation does not appear to produce meaningful differences in elbow flexor hypertrophy in untrained men over 10 weeks."; "with resistance profiles and elbow extension range of motion (ROM) matched between conditions."

## behind-back-cable-curl-2
- Pohimi: 11 vullnetarë, 80% e 1RM-së: EMG 111,46% (curl me dumbbell) kundër 93,39% (Bayesian), p = 0.003.
- Lidhja: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12550948/ (Parpa dhe kolegët, 2025, Muscles, doi 10.3390/muscles4040045)
- Provë (abstrakt): "Data from eleven volunteers ... were included in the analysis."; "each performed at 80% of their respective 1-RM"; "EMG amplitude (%) between the biceps curl (111.46 ± 26.80) and the Bayesian curl (93.39 ± 15.65)"; "the dumbbell biceps curl elicited significantly greater muscle activation compared to the Bayesian curl, suggesting that the conventional movement places a higher mechanical and neuromuscular demand on the biceps brachii."
- Shënim: abstrakti nuk e përkufizon "Bayesian curl". Përkufizimi (kabllo, shpatulla pas trupit ~50°) vjen nga Attarieh dhe kolegët, 2025 (PMC11906226). "% e MVC": abstrakti thotë MVC isometrik për normalizim, ndaj e lexoj "% e MVC".

## dumbbell-concentration-curl-1 (përmbledhje e WebFetch)
- Pohimi: 16 vullnetarë (8 burra, 8 gra), 70% e 1RM-së; concentration curl-i dha aktivizimin më të lartë të bicepsit.
- Lidhja: https://www.acefitness.org/prosourcearticle/4933/ace-study-reveals-best-biceps-exercises (ACE ProSource, gusht 2014)
- Provë (citate të WebFetch): "the concentration curl came out on top, eliciting significantly higher muscle activation of the biceps"; "researchers recruited 16 healthy, female and male volunteers (eight men and eight women)"; "the subjects used 70 percent of their 1-RM as resistance"; "The upside of doing a concentration curl is that you pretty much totally isolate the biceps."
- Ekspertët: byline "Scott Young, M.S., John P. Porcari, Ph.D., Clayton Camic, Ph.D., Attila Kovacs, Ph.D., and Carl Foster, Ph.D." (titulli sipas faqes së ACE-s; nuk e vërtetova gjetkë). Universiteti: UW-La Crosse sipas WebFetch. Viti 2014 sipas "ProSource, August 2014".
- Shënim: artikulli nuk jep përqindje EMG; fakti nuk ka numra të tillë.

## Ndreqjet pas kontrollit të pavarur
- barbell-curl-1: Coratella përdori Cable Tower (Technogym) me shufër për kapjet supinate/pronate dhe litar për neutralen (tekst i plotë, Europe PMC: "The biceps curl variations were performed using a Cable Tower ... connected to a bar ... for the supinated and pronated handgrip, and with a rope ... for the neutral handgrip"). Teksti, anglishtja dhe pyetja thonë tani "curl me kabllo", "gjatë fazës së ngritjes", "aktivizim", "+19% në nRMS". Abstrakti: "During the ascending phase, ... biceps brachii excitation was greater with the supinated compared to the pronated [+19(7)%...]".
- incline-cable-curl-1: teksti thotë qartë se u krahasuan dy curl me kabllo me një krah (shpatulla përpara = preacher, pas trupit = Bayesian), jo incline curl.
- one-arm-high-cable-curl-1: hoqa "muskuli mblidhet si top" (deformimi Popeye). Shënim: abstrakti te Europe PMC ka fjalinë "Consequently, the muscle forms a ball at the anterior mid-arm", por e hoqa sipas kërkesës. "More" tani thotë vetëm që është muskuli që punon te ky ushtrim.
- dumbbell-incline-curl-1: teksti thotë "incline biceps curl-i", pa "dumbbell"; te "more" shtova që burimi nuk e thotë se u bënë me dumbbell. Emri i temës mbetet siç është.
